Privacy

What we collect

Stash collects the business information you provide directly — sales, stock, expenses, and other records you write or photograph — along with basic account details like your name, phone number, and email.

How we use it

Your data is used to power your dashboard, answer your questions in chat, and generate insights about your business. We do not sell your business data to third parties.

Photos and notebook pages

Images you capture are processed to extract sales and stock information, then stored securely so you can review what Stash recorded.

Your control

You can request a copy of your data or ask for it to be deleted at any time from within the app or by contacting support.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@stash.app.
